# Woggle Presets

9 factory presets. Navigate to PRESET, rotate to select, long press to load.  
Any subsequent parameter change marks the preset as customised.

| # | Name | Character | Best used for |
|---|------|-----------|---------------|
| 1 | ClockedRnd | Clean clocked random, minimal bursts | Basic S&H, tame CV source |
| 2 | Woggle | Balanced fluid movement, moderate bursts | General musical patch companion |
| 3 | TotalChaos | Dense, unstable, aggressive | Experimental patches, heavy glitch |
| 4 | MelodicDrf | Slow smooth drift, low burst activity | Pitch or filter automation, droning |
| 5 | RatchetBug | Strong bursts, rhythmic articulation | Ratchets, drum triggers, gate cascades |
| 6 | GhostNotes | Sparse secondary events | Subtle accent triggers |
| 7 | DrunkenWlk | Wobbly, off-balance random walk | Human-feeling CV motion |
| 8 | BurstStorm | Maximum burst density, chaotic | Climax moments, percussive overload |
| 9 | AmbientTail | Slow, breathing, wide movement | Ambient drones, long slow timbres |

## Choosing a starting preset

- **Simple and predictable** → ClockedRnd or Woggle
- **Melodic / filter motion** → MelodicDrf or AmbientTail
- **Rhythmic impact** → RatchetBug or BurstStorm
- **Subtle enrichment** → GhostNotes or DrunkenWlk
- **Maximum chaos** → TotalChaos
